Hand tracking loads when you open the demo.

Three gestures.
Endless possibilities.

01

Index finger → draw

Raise only your index finger. Fold your middle, ring, and pinky fingers.

02

Pinky finger → erase

Raise only your pinky. Move it over a stroke to erase it.

03

Open hand → pause

Open your hand or make a fist to move without leaving a mark.

For the best tracking

Use one hand, good lighting, and a plain background. Keep your whole hand in view. Move slowly.

Video stays in your browser. No recording, upload, or microphone access.Closing the demo or leaving this tab stops the camera.

This browser demo uses MediaPipe hand tracking. The original application uses Qualcomm QIM SDK. Model files download from Google and jsDelivr when you open the demo.
